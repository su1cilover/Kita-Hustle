[ Before you sell anything ]

Ask a grown-up
first.

  • Ask a parent or guardian before you sell for real.
  • Sell to people you know, or with an adult nearby.
  • Meet buyers in public places, never alone at home.
  • Never send money first to a stranger or share your address or ID online.
  • Payment apps usually need an adult account. Use a parent's.
